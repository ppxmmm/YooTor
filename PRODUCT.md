# YooTor

## Register
Product: Thai estate-preparation workspace.

## Users and purpose
Owners preparing their estate and trusted family members organizing information after a death. Primary work covers tax preparation, life policies, will drafts and controlled document sharing.

## Brand personality
Calm, considerate, professional. Preserve the existing navy and teal identity and IBM Plex Sans Thai typography.

## Design principles
Clear task hierarchy, readable Thai text, visible labels, native accessible controls, responsive layouts and reduced-motion support. Favor restrained surfaces over ornamental dashboards. Preparation progress must never imply completed legal or tax action.

## References
Existing docs/design-system.md is the visual source of truth. Avoid dense nested panels, decorative metrics and low-contrast supporting copy.
